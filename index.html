About the Practice

Curious by nature.
Intentional by design.

I’m a multidisciplinary designer working at the intersection of Faith, Design, Media, and Tech. Over approximately ten years of practice, I’ve partnered with visionary founders, entrepreneurs, NGOs, and ministries across Nigeria and internationally.

My journey taught me that standing out online isn't the real problem — clarity is. When you have clarity beneath what you do, the design becomes an honest and inevitable reflection of that purpose.
