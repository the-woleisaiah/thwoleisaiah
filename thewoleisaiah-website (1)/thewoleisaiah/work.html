Featured Project

Atim Ekanem Foundation — Website Redesign

The foundation had a mission worth getting behind — but their digital presence wasn't doing it justice. The old site was difficult to navigate, visually inconsistent, and failed to communicate the depth of the work being done on the ground.

The redesign started with a single question: who needs to land here, and what do they need to feel and do? Donors needed to trust. Beneficiaries needed to find help quickly. Partners needed to understand the scope. The new site answered all three — clearly, consistently, without noise.
