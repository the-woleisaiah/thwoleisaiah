FAQ

Questions I get asked.

How long does a brand identity project take? +
A full brand identity project typically takes 3–6 weeks, depending on the scope and how quickly we move through the discovery and revision process. I work methodically — not rushed — because good brand work takes time to think through properly.
Do you work with clients outside Nigeria? +
Yes. I work with clients across Africa and globally. All project communication happens remotely via email, video calls, and shared design tools. Time zones are manageable — we'll figure out a schedule that works for both of us.
What's your design process like? +
Every project starts with discovery — understanding your business, your audience, and your goals. From there, I move into strategy before touching any design tool. Once the thinking is right, the design follows. I share concepts at key milestones and we refine from there. The process is collaborative, not a black box.
Do you offer payment plans? +
Yes. I understand that quality design is an investment, and I want to make it accessible for purpose-driven brands that are growing. I typically require a deposit to begin work, with the balance split across project milestones. We'll discuss the specifics in your proposal.
What if I only need one part of the service? +
That's completely fine. You don't need to take on everything at once. Whether you just need a logo, just a website, or just content templates — we can scope a project around exactly what you need right now, with room to expand later.
What makes your work different from other designers? +
I combine strategy with execution. Most designers focus on aesthetics; I start with the problem you're trying to solve. I also bring a faith-guided perspective that is uniquely valuable for ministries and purpose-driven brands. And I build systems — not one-time deliverables — so your brand can grow consistently over time.
