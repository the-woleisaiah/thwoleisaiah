Services / What I Bring to the Table

Thoughtful design.
Useful outcomes.

A connected set of design capabilities for organisations, founders, and ministries that need their identity, digital presence, and product experience to communicate with clarity and purpose.

01 / DISCIPLINE

Brand Identity & Strategy

Build a clear, memorable foundation for how your organisation looks, speaks, and is recognised across all physical and digital touchpoints.

Core Focus: Brand audit, strategic positioning, logo mark suites, color architectures, typography hierarchy, and comprehensive identity guidelines books.
Positioning · Logo Systems · Brand Guidelines · Color Systems
02 / DISCIPLINE

Web Design & Development

Thoughtful, conversion-engineered websites that clearly communicate your value proposition and turn visitors into active clients and supporters.

Core Focus: Information architecture, mobile-first responsive design, interactive prototypes, CMS integration (WordPress / Headless), and speed optimization.
Information Architecture · UI Design · Responsive Builds · CMS Setup
03 / DISCIPLINE

UI/UX & Product Design

Useful digital product experiences shaped around real human tasks, frictionless user flows, and ambitious business milestones.

Core Focus: User journey mapping, wireframing, high-fidelity Figma components, scalable design tokens, and developer handoff documentation.
User Flows · Wireframes · Design Systems · Interactive Prototypes
04 / DISCIPLINE

Creative Direction

A cohesive, disciplined visual point of view across campaigns, marketing collateral, social media touchpoints, and brand launches.

Core Focus: Art direction, campaign creative concepts, photo & video mood direction, brand tone-of-voice alignment, and multi-channel asset kits.
Art Direction · Campaign Concepts · Visual Systems · Asset Toolkits
05 / DISCIPLINE

Ministry & Faith-Based Branding

Spiritual depth expressed through intentional, contemporary visual language, broadcast graphics, sermon series identities, and conference media.

Core Focus: Ministry logo marks, church conference identities, digital display lower thirds, sermon artwork packages, and worship media systems.
Church Media Systems · Conference Branding · Broadcast Graphics
06 / DISCIPLINE

Design Education & Mentorship

Practical instruction and mentorship helping emerging graphic and product designers transition from making graphics to leading strategic design careers.

Core Focus: 1-on-1 portfolio reviews, strategic brand design frameworks, client communication training, and disciplined design workflows.
1-on-1 Mentorship · Masterclasses · Portfolio Reviews · Workshops
